## Notes

- **Research Policy** and **Science and Public Policy** are the most natural homes for the current framing (science-policy + intervention modelling).
- Research Policy requires a submission that is visibly anchored in **innovation studies** rather than purely economics or management, and a **data/code availability statement**.
- **Security Studies** / **Science & Global Security** become stronger candidates if the manuscript is extended with the quantum / dual-use technology case and explicitly discusses strategic competition.
- APCs marked “not publicly listed” are hybrid/Open-Select journals where the charge depends on institutional agreements and country; use the publisher’s cost finder before budgeting.
