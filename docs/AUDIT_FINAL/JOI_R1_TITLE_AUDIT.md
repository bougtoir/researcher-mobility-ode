# JOI R1 — Title Audit

- OLD: "Measuring research capacity through AI/ML researcher mobility: a dynamical model of community reproduction" (JOI-submission title)
- NEW (Option D): "Measuring research-community dynamics from bibliometric career transitions: evidence from global AI/ML"

Rationale: the editor's underlying concern is that the paper is about informetric measurement, not AI/ML. The new title front-loads the informetric contribution (bibliometric career transitions → community-level dynamics), relegates AI/ML to "evidence from", and drops the contested "research capacity" claim. OLD_TITLE is preserved in the build script; title page, abstract first line, cover letter and response document all carry the new title. No scientific claim changed.
